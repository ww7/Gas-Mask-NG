# Support and donation policy

Gas Mask NG is free software. Donations associated with this project are
voluntary and designated entirely for Ukraine's reconstruction, defense, and
support for people affected by Russia's war against Ukraine. They are not a
purchase, license fee, or payment for privileged access to the application.

The current Donate action opens [UNITED24](https://u24.gov.ua/donate). Donations
made there are processed by that destination, not by this repository or app.
Choose the campaign and beneficiary at the destination; the project does not
claim to have received or transferred those funds.

A future project-controlled support page may offer multiple clearly labelled
beneficiaries and a redirectable URL. No personal recipient, collection account,
redirect, or accounting claim is implied until published by the maintainer.
Any future project collection must follow the purpose above.

This policy does not change the GPL-2.0-or-later license or restrict anyone's
rights to use, modify, or redistribute the software. Original authorship and
third-party license notices remain in place.
